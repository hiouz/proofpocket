## Suggested form positioning

Project: ProofPocket

Primary track: Consumer Finance Apps

Special awards targeted: Best Walrus Memory; Best Use of Agent Payments

ProofPocket is the pre-signing policy and memory layer for autonomous machine payments. It gives agents bounded autonomy without treating remembered preference as unlimited authority. Walrus persists merchant outcomes, deterministic rules enforce human limits and replay protection, MPP settles a paid resource in USDC on Sui, and Tatum independently verifies the final transaction.
